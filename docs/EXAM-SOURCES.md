# Examination sources and verification — 9 November 2026

## LSAT

- [LSAC: types of questions](https://www.lsac.org/lsat/prepare/types-lsat-questions): current multiple-choice test has four 35-minute sections, including three scored and one unscored. Writing is separately administered.
- [LSAC: August 2027 changes](https://www.lsac.org/lsat-will-look-little-different-starting-august-2027): from August 2027, reading comprehension precedes two logical reasoning sections. Timing is 52, 45 and 45 minutes; question totals are 40, 32 and 32, including embedded unscored questions. Ten-minute breaks follow the first and second sections.
